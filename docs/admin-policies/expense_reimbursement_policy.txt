Expense & Reimbursement Policy

General Rule
Business expenses are reimbursed when they are reasonable, necessary
for work, and submitted with an itemized receipt within 30 days of
the expense date. Expenses submitted after 60 days are not
reimbursed except with director-level approval.

Travel Expenses
Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours; premium
economy is allowed for flights over 6 hours with manager approval.
Hotel bookings should stay within the per-city rate band published
on the Admin portal. Ground transport (taxi, rideshare, train) is
reimbursed at actual cost; personal car mileage is reimbursed at the
published company mileage rate.

Meals
Meals while travelling on approved business trips are reimbursed up
to a daily cap, published per country on the Admin portal, without a
detailed line-item requirement below that cap. Meals with clients or
candidates are reimbursed at actual cost with the attendee list noted
on the expense report.

Equipment & Office Supplies
A one-time home-office setup allowance is available for new fully
remote hires, claimable within the first 60 days of start date.
Ongoing office supplies (monitor, keyboard, chair) above the initial
allowance require manager pre-approval through the Admin portal
before purchase, not after.

Submission Process
All expenses are submitted through the Admin portal's Expenses tab
with the receipt attached as PDF or photo. Expenses over the
per-category threshold require a second approval from the relevant
department head in addition to the direct manager.

Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol outside of approved client entertainment, personal
entertainment, traffic or parking fines, and airline lounge or
loyalty program purchases are not reimbursable under any
circumstance.
