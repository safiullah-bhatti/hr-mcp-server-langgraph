Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to team lead
approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are
evaluated on a case-by-case basis depending on role and team needs.

Equipment Policy
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office stipend of
$300 for remote employees, covering desk, chair, or monitor purchases.
Equipment remains company property and must be returned upon exit.

Working Hours Policy
Core collaboration hours are 11 AM to 4 PM local time, during which
employees are expected to be reachable regardless of remote or office
status. Outside core hours, flexible scheduling is allowed as long as
weekly deliverables are met.

Relocation Policy
Employees relocating to a different city or country must inform HR
at least 60 days in advance, as this may affect tax residency, payroll
processing, and eligibility for certain benefits.
