Maternity Leave Policy
Female employees are entitled to 90 days of fully paid maternity leave.
An additional 30 days of unpaid leave may be requested through the HR
portal, subject to manager approval. Maternity leave can begin up to
2 weeks before the expected delivery date. Employees must notify HR
at least 30 days before the planned start of leave, where possible.

Paternity Leave Policy
Male employees are entitled to 10 days of fully paid paternity leave,
to be taken within 60 days of the child's birth. This leave does not
reduce the employee's annual leave balance.

Annual Leave Policy
All full-time employees accrue 20 paid annual leave days per year,
credited monthly at a rate of 1.66 days per month. Unused leave can be
carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year.
Leave requests should be submitted at least 5 working days in advance.

Sick Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 12 paid sick leave days per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Unused sick leave does not carry forward and is not paid out on exit.
